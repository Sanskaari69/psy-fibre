## 2 · Problem

- Eyebrow: `WHY NOBODY STAYS CONSISTENT`
- Statement: **Most people quit fibre at the glass, not the idea.**
- ¶1: Fibre usually comes as a tub and a scoop. Measure it, pour it into water, drink it before it thickens. Every one of those steps is a reason to skip a day.
- ¶2: Then there is the texture. A glass that turns to gel halfway down is hard to look forward to, and harder to repeat tomorrow.
- ¶3: And it needs a kitchen: a glass, a spoon, a tap. The routine breaks the first time you travel, work late or simply leave home.

## 3 · Format
